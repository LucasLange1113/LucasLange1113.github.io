I care about purposeful work and always having something new to learn. Outside of that, life is mostly sport, friends, family, and as much time outdoors as possible.

Get to know me↓
01 What shapes me

Learning keeps
me moving.

Through work, new places, and unfamiliar challenges.

I’m drawn to projects with tangible impact—work that turns ambiguity into something useful while giving me room to grow and learn. Away from the desk, I find the same sense of discovery in new places, different perspectives, and experiences that push me beyond what I already know.

Mentoring international students, organizing community life in Munich’s student residences, and facilitating workshops taught me to listen, take responsibility, and help people feel at home in a new group.

I’m always looking for something new to try. Most recently, that has meant indoor cycling and ultimate frisbee. Starting from scratch keeps me open-minded, adaptable, and willing to be surprised.

02 Away from the screen

Outside is where
I reset.

Hiking gives me distance, perspective, and the occasional unexpected trail companion. I like the simple rhythm of moving forward with only the next stretch of path to think about.

Rowing offers a different rhythm: technical, physical, and completely dependent on timing together. Both are a welcome counterweight to hours spent thinking in systems.
